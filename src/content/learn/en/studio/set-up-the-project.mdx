## Name it

Click the name field and type. It is what you will look for in the project list
later, so *Kitchen story* beats *Untitled video*.

## Choose the frame size

The **⊞** dropdown. Four sizes:

| Choice | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Reel / Story · 1080×1920** | Reels and Stories. The default |
| **Feed · 1080×1350** | A video post in the feed |
| **Square · 1080×1080** | A square post |
| **Landscape · 1920×1080** | Anything not going to Instagram |

Change it whenever you like — every clip re-fits to the new frame.

## Decide what fills the gaps

A clip that does not fill the frame leaves space. The next dropdown decides what
goes there:

- **Black bars** — plain black, top and bottom.
- **Blurred clip** — the clip itself, enlarged and blurred. Usually the best
  looking of the three.
- **Colour** — a flat colour you pick from the swatch beside it.

## Put your frame on it

On a 9:16 project a **🖼** dropdown appears. This is the branded frame from your
account's designs — the one drawn over every reel. Leave it on **Frame: account
default**, or choose **No frame** for this project only.

## Attach an account

The **Account** dropdown decides whose palette, frame and branding the project
uses, and where it posts. Set it early: the frame and the fonts follow it.
