## Add one

Put the playhead where it should appear, then press one of:

- **+ Sticker** — an emoji. A quick-pick strip offers 🔥 ❤️ ⭐ ✅ 👉 💡 📢 🎉 ⚡
  🏆 😂 👀, or type any emoji you like.
- **+ Shape** — **Rectangle**, **Ellipse**, **Line** or **Arrow**, with a corner
  radius, an outline-only option and a border width.
- **+ Logo** — your own logo, or any image URL. **📁 Pick image…** browses.
- **+ Progress bar** — a bar along the bottom that fills as the video plays.
  Good on anything long enough that a viewer wonders how much is left.

## Place it

**Drag it on the Program monitor.** The **Width**, **Height**, **X**, **Y**,
**Rotation**, **Opacity** and **Colour** fields in the Inspector are for
precision, not for placing it the first time.

Drag the block's ends on the Overlays lane to change when it is on screen.

## Before you export

Overlays are drawn in your browser. Export from the editor and they are in the
file; render on the server and they are not. The Inspector says so above the
controls. If an overlay matters, export from here.
