The video editor is where you cut, caption and finish a video. It is not in the
left rail, and the thing in the rail that looks like it is, is not.

## Before you start

The editor runs in **Chrome or Edge**. Safari and Firefox cannot open it at all.
The first time you use it you will be asked to choose a **folder on your own
disk** for your media — pick a plain folder, not Desktop, Documents or
Downloads, and not one that syncs to Dropbox or iCloud.

## Open it

1. Click **Visual Studio** in the left rail, under Tools. This opens the *photo*
   editor — that is expected.
2. At the top of that page, click the **Video ↗** tab.
3. You land on the project list. Click **+ New Video Project**.

It asks nothing. You get an empty 1080×1920 project called *Untitled video*, and
you set the size later from the bar at the top.

## Pick up where you left off

Two ways back into a project you already started:

- The same **Video ↗** tab lists your drafts under *Or resume a draft*, each
  marked **exported** or **in progress**.
- **Activity Log** has a **Video Editor Projects** table at the bottom. Press
  **▶ Resume** on a row.

## It saves itself

Every fifteen seconds, and whenever you leave. The bar at the top says **Saved**
and the time. You can also press **💾 Save** whenever you want to be sure.
