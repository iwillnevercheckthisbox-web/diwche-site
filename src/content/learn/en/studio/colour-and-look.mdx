## Grade it

The **Color** block, in the order colourists use them:

- **Lift** — the darks.
- **Gamma** — the midtones, which is where faces live.
- **Gain** — the brights.
- **Saturation** and **Hue**.

Small moves. A ±10 change on Gamma is already visible.

## Or just use a filter

The **Filter** strip has two dozen looks — Mono, Warm, Cool, Vivid, Fade,
Clarendon, Moon, Noir, Cinema, Golden hour and the rest. Click one, then set
**Intensity** to dial it back.

**Adjust** underneath adds **Temperature**, **Tint**, **Vignette** and **Grain**.

There are also two one-press buttons: **◐ Black & white** and **🎨 Restore
colour**.

## Make every clip match

This is the useful part.

1. Grade one clip until it looks right.
2. **Copy look**.
3. **Apply look to all clips** — or select another clip and **Paste look**.

**Reset** puts a clip back to untouched.

## Green screen

The **Key & mask** block:

1. Turn on **Chroma key (green screen)**.
2. Press **Green** or **Blue**, or pick the exact colour with the swatch.
3. Raise **Tolerance** until the background disappears.
4. Raise **Softness** to stop the edge looking cut out with scissors.
5. **Spill removal** takes the green reflected onto hair and shoulders.

## Mask a shape

**Mask** gives you **Rectangle** or **Ellipse**, with **Inset**, **Corner
radius**, **Feather** and **Invert**. Useful for a spotlight, or for hiding a
corner of the frame.

## One thing to know before you export

Filter, temperature, tint, vignette and grain are drawn **in your browser**. The
editor says so under the strip. Export from the editor and they are in the file;
send the project to the server to render and they are not. If a look matters,
export it here.
