Sound lives on three lanes: **A1** music, **A2** sound effects and voice-over,
**A3** the clips' own audio.

## Put music under it

1. Open the **🎵 Audio** tab of the Inspector. The **Audio Track** card is
   always there, whether or not anything is selected.
2. Choose from **Pick music…** and press **+ Add**, or **🎵 Upload** your own
   (MP3, WAV or M4A).
3. It lands on **A1**. Drag it to where it should start; drag its ends to trim.

**🔊 SFX** beside it opens the effects — camera shutter, whoosh, hits, ding,
clicks, UI, riser. Those go on **A2**.

## Set the level

Select the music item. In the Inspector:

- **Vol** for the whole item.
- **Fade In** and **Fade Out**, with **Linear** or **Exponential**. Exponential
  sounds more natural on a music fade.
- **Volume automation** — the same **◆** keyframe widget as the picture, so you
  can drop the level under one line and bring it back after.

## Duck it under speech

Instead of automating by hand, turn on **Ducking**:

> Lower this under speech (voice-over and the video's own sound)

Then set **Amount** (how far it drops), **Sensitivity** (how easily speech
triggers it) and **Fade** (how quickly it moves). This is the single biggest
improvement to a video where someone is talking over music.

## Cut to the beat

In **Level & beats**:

1. **⚖ Normalise** — brings the track to a sensible level.
2. **♪ Detect beats** — marks them along the item, and timeline drags snap to
   them from then on.
3. **✂ Cut clips to beats** — chops Video 1 at the beats.

## Shape it

**Mix** sets the **Lane**, **Pan**, and **Mute** / **Solo** / **Loop**.

**Effects** adds up to seven: **📞 Phone / Radio**, **🔁 Echo**, **🏛 Reverb**,
**🎛 Equalizer**, **📣 Megaphone**, **🌊 Underwater**, **🤖 Robot**. Each gets
its own parameters, and **▲ ▼** reorder them — order changes the result.
