This is text **you type**. Captions transcribed from speech are a different
thing on their own lane — see [Subtitles](/learn/subtitles/transcribe-inside-the-editor/).

## Add it

1. Put the playhead where the text should appear.
2. Press **+ Add Text at Playhead** under the timeline.

<Shot name="ve-add-row" callouts={[
  { x: 30, y: 93, text: 'Adds text at the playhead' },
]} />

A block appears on the **Text** lane and the Inspector opens on **🔤 Text**.

## Write and place it

Type into the box — **Enter** makes a new line. Then **drag the text itself on
the Program monitor** to position it. That is the fastest way; the X and Y
numbers in the Inspector are there for when you want two titles to line up
exactly.

**Start** and **Duration** set when it is on screen, or drag the block's ends on
the lane.

## Style it

**Style** gives you eight presets — Classic, Strong, Modern, Neon, Typewriter,
Editorial, Label, Caption. Start from one, then adjust:

- Font, colour and **Size**; **B**, **I**, **CAPS**.
- **Wrap width** and **Line height**; align Left, Center or Right.
- **Background** — **No background**, **Tight**, **Pill**, or **Per-line**, with
  a colour and **Opacity**. A background is what makes text readable over busy
  footage.
- **Stroke & Shadow** — an outline and a drop shadow. A 2px stroke in the
  opposite colour does more for legibility than any amount of shadow.

**Palette** at the top pulls the colours and fonts from your account's palette,
so a title matches everything else you post without picking anything.

## Animate it

**Position & Animation**: **In** and **Out** each offer *fade*, *slide up*,
*slide down*, *slide left*, *slide right* and *scale pop*. Fade in, fade out is
right most of the time.
