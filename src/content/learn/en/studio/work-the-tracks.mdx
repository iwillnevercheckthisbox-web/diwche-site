## The lanes, top to bottom

| Lane | What goes on it |
| --- | --- |
| **Text** | Titles and captions you typed. Drag the text itself on the Program monitor to place it |
| **Subtitles** | Transcribed captions. Appears only once there are cues |
| **Overlays** | Stickers, shapes, logos, the progress bar. Appears only once you add one |
| **Effects** | Zooms, pans and tilts. Applies to whatever is under it |
| **Video 2** | Anything laid *over* the main sequence. Hidden until you use it |
| **Video 1** | The main sequence. Cuts, transitions, the spine of the video |
| **A1** | Music |
| **A2** | Sound effects and voice-over |
| **A3** | The clips' own sound |

**Three of them are invisible until they have something on them** — Video 2,
Overlays and Subtitles. A fresh project shows four lanes, not seven, and nothing
is missing.

## Add Video 2

A ghost lane sits above Video 1 with a **+ V2** button on it. Click that, or just
drag a pool item onto the strip marked **Drop here for Video 2**.

Use it for a cutaway over someone talking, a logo bug, or a picture-in-picture.

## Get one lane out of the way

Every lane header has a **🔓** that turns into **🔒**. A locked lane cannot be
dragged, trimmed or deleted — the usual reason to lock Video 1 while you fiddle
with titles above it.

The three audio lanes have two more:

- **M** mutes that lane.
- **S** solos it — everything else goes quiet.

Beside them is a volume slider for the whole lane, separate from any one clip's
volume.
