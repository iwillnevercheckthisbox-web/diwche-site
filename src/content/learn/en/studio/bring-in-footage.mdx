Everything you can use lives in the **Media Pool** down the left side. Nothing
is on the timeline until you put it there.

## Four ways in

1. **📁 Upload** — choose files from your computer. Video is best as MP4; images
   can be JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, BMP or TIFF.
2. **🖼 Upload from Gallery** — anything you have already made or saved in
   Diwche.
3. **A YouTube link** — paste it in the *YouTube link…* field and press **+**.
   Two checkboxes decide what comes with it: **Keep audio**, and **Subtitles**
   if you want its captions transcribed.
4. **An image or video URL** — paste and press **+**.

## Get it onto the timeline

Three ways, and they do different things:

- **Double-click** a pool item — it goes on the end of **Video 1**.
- **Drag** it onto a track — it lands where you drop it.
- **Select** it, then use the Inspector: **+ Add to Video 1** puts it in the main
  sequence, **+ Add to Video 2 (overlay)** puts it *over* whatever is already
  there.

## Try it

Upload three clips. Double-click each in turn. You now have a three-clip
sequence on Video 1 with a cut between each pair — and each of those cuts is a
transition you can change.
