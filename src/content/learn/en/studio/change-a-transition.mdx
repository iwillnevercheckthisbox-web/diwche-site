Every cut on Video 1 already **is** a transition. You never add one — you change
what the one that is already there does.

That is why there is no "add transition" button, and why looking for one is the
usual way people get stuck here.

## Find the cut

Where two clips meet on Video 1 there is a **◆** — a small diamond sitting in a
full-height strip.

## Change it

1. **Click the ◆.** The Inspector on the right jumps to its **✨ Effects** tab by
   itself.
2. Pick a transition from the list.
3. Set **Duration** with the slider underneath.

## The eleven

| Transition | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **◐ Cross Dissolve** | One shot fades through into the next. The safe one |
| **◎ Iris** | A circle opens out of the new shot |
| **↷ Jump Cut** | A hard cut, no blend. This is the default |
| **⚡ Smash Cut** | A hard cut with a jolt |
| **⬛ Dip to Black** | Out to black, then in again. Reads as time passing |
| **▥ Wipe** | A hard edge travels across |
| **⬅ Slide left** | The new shot pushes in from the right |
| **➡ Slide right** | The new shot pushes in from the left |
| **⬆ Push up** | The new shot pushes in from below |
| **🔍 Zoom through** | Pushes into one shot and out of the next |
| **〰 Blur dissolve** | Blurs out and back in. Softer than a dissolve |

## How long

**Duration** runs from **0.2** to **3** seconds and starts at **0.5**. Two
things worth knowing:

- The transition *overlaps* the two clips, so a long one makes the video
  slightly shorter.
- Under about 0.3 seconds most of them stop reading as anything but a cut.

For an ordinary dissolve between two talking shots, 0.4–0.6 is right. For a dip
to black between two scenes, 1 second or more.

## Go back to a plain cut

Select the **◆** and pick **↷ Jump Cut**. You cannot delete a transition,
because there is always a cut there — a plain cut *is* Jump Cut.

## Move the cut without changing the length

Drag the **strip**, not the diamond. One clip gains exactly what the other
loses, the cut moves, and the video stays the same length. This is a *roll*, and
it is how you fix a cut that lands a beat too early.

Two targets on the same few pixels: **the diamond selects, the strip rolls.**

## Only on Video 1

Video 2 and the audio lanes have the same draggable strip where two items touch,
and it rolls the same way — but no diamond, and no transition. Transitions
belong to the main sequence.
