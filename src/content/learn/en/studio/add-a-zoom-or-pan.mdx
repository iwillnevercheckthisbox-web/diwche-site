An effect sits on its own **Effects** lane and applies to whatever is underneath
it — video or photo, Video 1 or Video 2. That is the point: you can stretch one
push-in across a cut, and you never touch the clips.

## Add one

1. Put the playhead where the movement should start.
2. Press **+ Add Effect at Playhead**.
3. Drag its ends to cover exactly as much as you want.

## Set the movement

The Inspector opens on **✨ Effects**, with three pairs:

- **Zoom (push in/out)** — **From** and **To** as a percentage. 100 → 112 over
  four seconds is a slow push. 112 → 100 pulls back.
- **Pan (horizontal)** — From and To across.
- **Tilt (vertical)** — From and To up and down.

Set one pair and leave the others alone; two at once usually reads as a mistake.

**Easing** decides how it accelerates: **linear** is mechanical, **easeInOut**
is what a camera operator would do. Use easeInOut unless you want the
mechanical feel.

## The other kinds

The **kind** dropdown also has **rotate**, **blur**, **shake**, **vignette**,
**colorGrade** and **glitch**. Each takes the same From/To pair.

**Shake** on a hard cut and **glitch** on a beat both do a lot in a quarter of a
second and far too much in two.

## Try it

Drop a still photo on Video 1. Give it four seconds. Add an effect over the
whole of it, Zoom From 100 To 110, easing easeInOut. A still photograph now
reads as a shot.
