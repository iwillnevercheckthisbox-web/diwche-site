Your Direction only changes when you change it. This is the quick way to change
it — talk, instead of editing five fields.

<Shot name="ideas-talk" label="Talk to me" />

## Do it

1. On **Ideas**, press **● Talk to me** in the header.
2. Press the round button and say what has changed. Up to two minutes. Or
   **Type instead**.
3. Press **Tell him**.

Say things like *I have started covering restoration, and I have stopped doing
artist biographies*, or *the reels about process do much better than the ones
about history*.

It needs a sentence or two — under about twenty characters and the button stays
disabled.

## Approve the change

It comes back on the **Today** tab as a banner:

> 💡 …the change it wants to make

with **Yes, do it** and **Not now**.

It proposes **one small change** and asks first. Nothing is overwritten, and
declining costs nothing.

## When to use it instead of editing

Use **Talk to me** when something has shifted and you cannot immediately say
which field it belongs in. Use **✎ Edit the Direction** at the foot of the page
when you know exactly what you want to change.
