The **Market** tab is about everyone else: the accounts you listed as peers,
what has done unusually well for them lately, and what people are asking about
around your subjects.

<Shot name="ideas-market" label="Ideas · Market" />

## The benchmark

A row of figures across the top: how many peer accounts, how many posts between
them in 30 days, their median likes per post, and how many breakouts.

The median is the useful one. It tells you what normal looks like at your size,
which is the only way to know whether your own numbers are good.

## Breakouts

A breakout is a post that did unusually well **for that account** — at least two
robust standard deviations above its own 30-day median, and no more than
fourteen days old.

That definition matters: it is not "a post with a lot of likes". A big account's
ordinary post is not a breakout. A small account's surprise is.

Each shows the thumbnail, the handle, **N× their usual**, the numbers and the
caption, with three buttons: **Open on Instagram**, **Carousel from this**, and
**Reel from this**. The last two open the right studio with the idea already in
it.

## This week's themes

**✦ Mine themes** reads the breakouts and the wider conversation and gives you
the subjects that are resonating right now, each with a score and the signals
behind it. It also feeds the weekly plan.

## What people are asking

A line names where the conversation is read from — X and Reddit — and whether
each is reachable. It is **research only**. Diwche never posts there.

## If it is empty

You need peers. **Manage peers →** goes to the Scraper page, and an account
needs at least four scraped posts before it has a baseline to compare against.
