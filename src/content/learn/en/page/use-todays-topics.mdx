| On the card | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Format pill** | Post, Carousel, Story or Reel — what it suits |
| **Pillar** | Which of your subjects it belongs to |
| **⏱ Why now** | Why today rather than any day. The bit worth reading |
| **Title and hook** | The idea, and the line that would open it |
| **📖 Fact anchor** | Something checkable, so it is not just an opinion |
| **Source** | The trusted place it leans on |

## Do something with it

**✦ Make** — asks *Make as* and offers **Carousel**, **Reel**, **Post** and
**Story**, with its own suggestion highlighted. Pick one and the right studio
opens with the brief already filled in.

**🔖 Keep** — parks it on the **Kept** list for a day when you have no time.
Press again to un-keep.

**✕ Not this ▾** — and say why: **Done it**, **Off-brand**, **Boring**, **Too
close to recent**. The reason matters more than the dismissal; it is how
tomorrow's list gets better.

## Three lists

**Today** · **Kept** · **Made**. On Made, each topic shows how it did — *did 1.4×
your usual*, or *posted · measuring…* — and **Open again** takes you back to it.

## Ask for more

**10 more** thinks up another ten. The first batch of the day takes about a
minute.

## Say whether it was any good

Once a day: **Were today's topics good?** with **👍** and **👎**. Thumbs down
opens a note box, and the note is the useful part —

> too generic — I want named people and dates

is worth ten silent thumbs down. Change your mind later with **Change**.
