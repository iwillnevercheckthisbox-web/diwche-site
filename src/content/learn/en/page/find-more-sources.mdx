Under **This week** there is a fold: *More sources — articles, videos and photos
matched to your keywords*. Open it.

## Set what it looks for

The **Content Discovery** section decides everything below it.

**Content Mode**

- **News** — breaking articles from the feeds set up on this account.
- **Evergreen** — how-to and informational pieces that do not date.

**Photo Source**

- **Stock photos (Unsplash)**
- **Art & museum archive (Wikimedia Commons)**

**Content Brief** — the one that does the work. There is a template; fill it in:

```
Topic/Niche: …
Audience: …
Content types: …
Tone: …
Prioritize: …
Avoid: …
```

Then **Save**.

Worth knowing: **your account's name is never used to decide relevance.** Only
the keywords and this brief are. An account called *Art Notes* gets nothing from
its own name — it gets what you write here.

## Keywords

Add the words your subject is actually written about under. Chips with an **✕**
to remove. Be specific: *Persian calligraphy* finds things; *art* finds
everything and therefore nothing.

## Read the suggestions

**Today's Suggestions** comes in three groups: **Articles**, **YouTube** and
**Photos**. Each refreshes on its own — **🔄 Refresh Articles** does not clear
your photos.

Each card has a title, a snippet and its source. Photos add **⬇ Download** and
**🖌 Edit**. Everything has **Dismiss**.

A refresh takes 20–60 seconds.

## Where they turn up again

These suggestions feed the **📋 Pick from Suggestions** dropdown in Article
Carousel and Reels Studio, so anything you find here is one click from being a
post.
