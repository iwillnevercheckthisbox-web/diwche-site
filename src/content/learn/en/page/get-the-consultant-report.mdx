The report is the one part of Performance that tells you what to *do*. It is
generated every Monday night, or now.

<Shot name="performance-report" label="This week's consultant report" />

## Get it

Open **Performance**, find **This week's consultant report**, press **✦ Generate
report**. It becomes **⏳ Working…**, then **↻ Regenerate** once there is one.

## Read it

**Findings** — statements with the evidence in italics under each. What actually
happened this week.

**Stop** — things to stop doing, when there are any. Worth reading first; it is
the shortest list and the easiest to act on.

**Actions** — each tagged with what kind it is. Things you control.

**This week's experiment** — a hypothesis, an **A** and a **B**, and what to
measure and when. One experiment, not five, so the result means something.

Below it, **last week's experiment** and its verdict.

## The peer line

Underneath: how your engagement compares with the peer accounts you follow —
their median likes, how many breakouts they had, and your own percentage.

## If it will not generate

> Not enough data yet — a few posts with a full 14-day lifecycle, a couple of
> days of account insights, or some peer accounts on the Scraper page will
> unlock the report.

Any one of those three unlocks it. The quickest is usually adding peers.
