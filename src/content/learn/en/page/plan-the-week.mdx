Where Today gives you something to post now, **This week** gives you a plan with
the reasoning attached.

<Shot name="ideas-week" label="Ideas · This week" />

## Get one

Open **Ideas → This week** and press **✦ Plan this week**. It is also generated
by itself every Monday night. Once there is a plan the button becomes **↻
Regenerate**.

## Read an idea

Each card carries more than a topic card does:

- **Format** and **origin** — *proven for you*, *peer breakout*, *in the news*,
  *follow-up* or *evergreen*.
- **A fit score** — a bare number. How well it matches your Direction and what
  has worked for you.
- **A suggested hour**, and which character should write it.
- **Why now**, in a sentence.
- **Evidence** — the actual figures it is arguing from.
- **Sources** you can open and check.
- **Suggested hashtags**.

The evidence is the part to read. An idea that says *proven for you* with a
number attached is worth more than one that just sounds good.

## Act on it

**✦ Create** opens the right studio, filled in, and marks the idea used.

**📌 Plan** parks it as planned without starting it.

**Dismiss** asks a reason first — **Not relevant**, **Already did**, **Wrong
format** — then **✕ Dismiss**. **Show N dismissed** at the foot brings them back
into view.

Once a planned idea has been posted and measured, the card shows *result 1.4×
your usual*.
