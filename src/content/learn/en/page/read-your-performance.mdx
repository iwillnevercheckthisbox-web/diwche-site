## Where the account stands

Five figures — followers, reach, profile views, accounts engaged, average
engagement — each with the change against the week before.

**Read the change, not the level.** A number on its own says almost nothing; 9.5K
followers is neither good nor bad. +132 in seven days is information.

## Growth

Followers as a big line, then reach, profile views and accounts engaged as three
small charts underneath, all zero-based.

These come from Instagram's own account insights, captured nightly. **Capture
today** at the top takes a reading immediately, which is how you start the line
on your first day rather than waiting until tomorrow.

Two charts that answer different questions and often disagree:

- **Followers online, by hour** — when your audience is on Instagram. Champagne
  bars mark the hours you are already scheduled to post.
- **Your average reach, by hour published** — when the posts you have already
  made actually did well.

Trust the second one. The first is availability; the second is evidence.

Each has a **Table** button if you would rather read numbers.

**If the left chart is empty**, Instagram only provides it above roughly 100
followers. Nothing is broken.

## Best and worst

**Top 5** and **Bottom 5**, ranked by how far each post beat or missed *your own
usual numbers* — not by raw size, which mostly measures how many followers you
had that week.

Each row has the engagement rate, an **N×** badge, and a sparkline of
interactions over fourteen days.

## What worked, and why

Every post read three days after it went out, compared with your own history and
explained in plain words. Each card carries a verdict — **Worked**,
**Underperformed** or **Mixed** — and chips for *sent on*, *saved*, *watched*
and *engaged*.

**Why we're saying this** on each card names the rules that matched. These are
fixed rules measured against your history, not a guess, which is why you can
argue with one.

A post needs its three days. A page with four posts this week has nothing here
yet.

## What's actually working

Your posts grouped by what they were and when: **by source**, **by persona**,
**by media type**, **by posting hour**, **by hook type**, **by topic**, **by
caption length**, **by palette**, **by template**.

A group needs **at least 3 posts**, and each post has to have finished its
14-day window. This is the section that fills in slowly and then becomes the
most useful one on the page.

## Action plan

**✨ Generate** writes a plan where every recommendation cites a real figure from
your own history. It needs about ten finished posts.
