Ideas stays empty until this is done. It takes about five minutes, most of it
talking.

## Open it

**Ideas** in the left rail, under Your page. Pick your account. If there is no
Direction yet, the three-step setup opens instead of the tabs:

**Your page** → **Accounts you admire** → **Your page so far**

## Step 1 — talk about the page

Press the round **●** and talk for up to three minutes, the way you would
explain the page to a friend. The prompts on screen are what to cover:

- Who is it for, and where do they live?
- What will you post about — and what should it never be?
- Where are you? Your city or region.
- What can you show that others cannot?
- What do you believe that others in your field will not say out loud?
- Will you be on camera?
- What do you want someone to do after they see a post?

Press **■** when you are done. Do not script it; half-sentences are fine.

**Prefer to type?** **Type instead** under the recorder. A few sentences is
plenty.

### Check what it heard

**What Diwche heard** shows the transcript in an editable box. Fix anything
wrong here — this is the text the Direction gets written from, so a misheard word
becomes a misunderstood page.

Then **Next →**.

## Step 1b — check the Direction

It comes back with a draft:

| Field | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Your audience** | Who you are for |
| **Your promise** | What they get from following |
| **Your pillars** | The two to eight subjects you return to |
| **Your voice** | How the page talks |
| **Never** | Subjects and angles you will not touch |

Change anything. **You need at least two pillars** before you can continue.

Under **What only you can make** there is more, and it is worth filling in — it
is what stops suggestions being generic: where you are, what you can show that
others cannot, what you believe that others will not say, what you want people
to do, whether you will be on camera, and any series you want to run.

**Looks right ✓**.

## Step 2 — accounts you admire

Up to ten Instagram pages that do well what you want to do. Type a handle or
paste a link, press Enter.

Each is read **once** — a few cents a page — and not read again unless you press
**↻**. Diwche learns what works for them. It does not copy them.

**Skip** if you have none in mind; you can add them later.

## Step 3 — your page so far

If Instagram is connected, **Read my posts** goes through your own back
catalogue and learns what your followers already respond to. If it is not
connected, skip — nothing is blocked.

Then **Meet Diwche →**.

## Afterwards

Your Direction sits at the foot of the Ideas page, in a fold marked **Your
Direction — version N**.

**✎ Edit the Direction** reopens the card. It only ever changes when you change
