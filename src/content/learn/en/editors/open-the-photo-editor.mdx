## Before you start

**Chrome or Edge.** Safari and Firefox cannot open it.

The first time, it asks for a **folder on your own disk** to keep your media in.
Pick a plain folder — not Desktop, Documents or Downloads, and not one that syncs
to Dropbox or iCloud Drive. Files there are the only copy, so put it somewhere
you back up.

## Open it

**Visual Studio** in the left rail, under Tools. That is the photo editor,
whatever the nav item is called.

Two other doors reach the same editor at a different size:

| Door | Size |
| --- | --- |
| **Visual Studio** | Whatever the photo is |
| **Story Studio** — from Stories → 🎨 Design a story | 1080×1920 |
| **Post Studio** — from Create → Post → Single Post | Feed size |

**It is one editor.** Same tools, same rail, same everything; only the artboard
differs. If you have learned one, you have learned all three.

## The tool rail

<Shot name="pe-open" label="The tool rail" />

Down the left, in order:

1. **Crop**
2. **Adjust**
3. **Remove background**
4. **Filters**
5. **Text**
6. **Layers**
7. **Templates**
8. **Publish**

Click one and its panel opens beside it. Your work is kept when you switch tools.

## Undo

**Ctrl/Cmd + Z** and **Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z**, throughout.
