## Add it

<Shot name="pe-text" label="Text" />

1. Pick **Text** in the rail.
2. Add a text object and type.
3. Drag it on the picture to place it. Drag a corner to resize.

## Style it

Font, size, colour, alignment, weight and spacing are in the panel. Your
account's palette fonts are there, and so is anything you uploaded on the
**Fonts** page.

## Make it readable

Text on a photograph fails for one reason: not enough contrast against what is
behind it. Three fixes, in order of how well they work:

1. **Put it on a plain part of the picture.** Sky, a wall, a shadow.
2. **Give it a background** — a solid or semi-transparent panel behind the words.
3. **Give it an outline or a shadow.** An outline in the opposite colour survives
   any background; a soft shadow only helps on mid-tones.

If you are using a dark shadow on a dark photo, none of it is working. Move the
text instead.

## Keep it short

A photo post is read at a glance while scrolling. Six words at a size you can
read from arm's length beat a sentence at half the size.
