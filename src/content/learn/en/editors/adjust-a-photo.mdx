**Adjust** is the second tool. Every dial has a middle at neutral, and dragging
either way moves it from there.

<Shot name="pe-adjust" label="Adjust" />

## The three that do most of the work

1. **Exposure** — overall brightness. Fix this before anything else.
2. **Contrast** — the gap between dark and light. A small amount makes almost any
   phone photo look better.
3. **Saturation** — how strong the colours are. Move it in small steps; the
   distance between rich and garish is short.

## The rest

- **Shadows** and **Highlights** — recover a dark foreground or a blown sky
  without touching the middle.
- **Temperature** and **Tint** — warm/cool, and green/magenta. Temperature fixes
  indoor lighting.
- **Sharpen** — a little, at the end. Too much makes edges crunchy.
- **Blur**, **Vignette**, **Grain** and the rest — effects rather than
  corrections.

## The order that works

Exposure → Shadows and Highlights → Contrast → Temperature → Saturation →
Sharpen.

Doing it in that order means each move is made against a picture that is already
roughly right, so you make fewer of them.

## Or skip it

**Filters** — the next tool down — does all of this in one press with a ready-made
look. Adjust is for when a filter is nearly right and one thing is off, or when
the photo needs fixing rather than styling.
