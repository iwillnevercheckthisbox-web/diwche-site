## Your options

- **Download** — the image to your computer. Choose the format and the scale.
- **Save to Gallery** — into Diwche, where every studio can use it. Do this even
  when you are downloading: it is how the picture becomes available to a carousel
  slide or a video later.
- **Post** — to Instagram, through the Activity Log.

## Format and scale

**JPEG** for photographs — smaller, and Instagram re-compresses everything
anyway. **PNG** when the image has transparency or flat areas of colour and text,
where JPEG shows artefacts around the letters.

Scale up only if you are handing the file to someone else. Instagram will not
show more than about 1080 across.

## Where it goes when you post

Not straight to Instagram — into the **Activity Log** as **Pending Review**.

<Shot name="activity-log" label="Activity Log" />

Approve it there, or schedule it for a time. See
[Work the Activity Log](/learn/manage/work-the-activity-log/).
