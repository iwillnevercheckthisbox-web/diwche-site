**Layers** is the sixth tool. Everything you have added — the photo, each piece
of text, every shape — is a layer, and the panel lists them.

<Shot name="pe-layers" label="Layers" />

## The list is the stacking order

Top of the list draws on top. Drag a layer up or down to change what covers what.

## Per layer

- **Hide** it, to see what is underneath while you work.
- **Lock** it, so you stop dragging the background by accident. Do this to the
  photo early.
- **Opacity**, to fade one back.
- **Delete**.

## Two things worth composing this way

**Text behind a subject.** Cut out the subject with
[Remove background](/learn/editors/remove-the-background/), put the text on a
layer *below* it, and the subject overlaps the words.

**A colour wash.** A semi-transparent rectangle in a palette colour, between the
photo and the text, makes any text readable on any photograph. Set its opacity to
around 30–40%.
