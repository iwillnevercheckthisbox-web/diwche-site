## Do it

<Shot name="pe-removebg" label="Remove background" />

1. Pick **Remove background** in the rail.
2. Press the button in the panel.
3. Wait — it works on the server, and takes a few seconds.

The background becomes transparent, and the subject stays.

## What it is good at

A clear subject against a distinct background — a person, an object, a bottle, a
building. Hair and fur are where any tool of this kind struggles, and where you
should look before trusting the result.

## What to do with it afterwards

The point is usually one of these:

- Put the subject over a flat colour from your palette, so a snapshot becomes a
  designed post.
- Put it over another picture, using [Layers](/learn/editors/work-with-layers/).
- Put text **behind** the subject — add the text on a layer below it and the
  subject overlaps the words. It looks considerably more designed than it is.

## If it takes the wrong thing

Try cropping tighter first so the subject is the obvious content of the frame,
then run it again.
