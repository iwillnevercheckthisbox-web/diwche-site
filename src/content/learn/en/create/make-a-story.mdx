Stories does two different jobs: it re-posts your best recent work to your story
on a schedule, and it lets you design one from nothing.

## Set it to run itself

1. **Stories** in the left rail, and pick your account.
2. Under **Post one story at each**, add the times you want. **+ Add** for each.
3. Set the **Candidate pool size** — how many recent posts it ranks by reach
   before choosing.
4. **Save Settings**.

**Each configured time posts exactly one story** — the highest-reach post from
yesterday that has not already gone out. So four times means four stories spread
across the day, not four at once.

## Post one now

**📖 Post One Now (auto-pick)** takes the best candidate immediately.

## Choose one yourself

**🖼️ Choose from Last 7 Days** opens a picker, ranked by reach, showing each
candidate's numbers and whether it has already been used. Add a line in **Text to
overlay**, then **Post this** — or **Post again** on one that has gone out
before.

## Design one from scratch

**🎨 Design a story** opens Story Studio — the photo editor at story size, with
your account's story design and palette. See
[the photo editor](/learn/editors/open-the-photo-editor/).

## Afterwards

**History** at the foot lists every story with its reach and status.

## One thing to do yourself

Stickers, polls, music and links are added in Instagram after the story is up.
Diwche tells you which ones suit the post; you tap them on your phone.
