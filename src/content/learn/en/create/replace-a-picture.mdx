Press **🔄 Replace** on a slide. The panel offers five sources, in this order.

<Shot name="carousel-sources" label="Article Carousel" />

## 1. Article photos

The photographs downloaded from the article itself, if you ticked **Use images
from the article** when you generated it. A thumbnail grid — click one.

These are the best option when they exist: they are actually about the subject.

## 2. Suggested photos

Pictures Diwche has already found for your keywords, from the Suggestions on the
Ideas page.

## 3. Search photos

A query box and a source:

- **Unsplash** — modern stock photography
- **Wikimedia Commons** — an art and museum archive

Search terms that describe a *scene* work better than ones that name a concept:
*Tehran skyline at dusk* beats *urban development*.

## 4. From URL

Paste a direct image address and press **Use URL**.

## 5. Upload file

**Choose JPEG / PNG…**, then **Use File**.

## Reframe without replacing

**🔍 Adjust** on a slide that has a source photo lets you move and scale the
picture inside its frame — for when the right image is cropped wrongly.
