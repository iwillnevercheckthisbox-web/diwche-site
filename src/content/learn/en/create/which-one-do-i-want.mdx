Start from what you already have. That decides the studio, not the other way
round.

| You have | Use | Which opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A link to an article | **Article Carousel** | A swipeable post from the page |
| A PDF or an image | **Article Carousel** | The same, from the file |
| A topic and some numbers | **Data Infographic** | Stat slides |
| One picture to design | **Single Post** | The photo editor at feed size |
| Footage you filmed | **D1 Studio** | It cuts and captions it |
| An idea and a camera | **D1 Studio** | It writes a script to read |
| An idea and no camera | **Reel** | Scripted and voiced for you |
| Something to put on your story | **Story** | Story tools |
| Feeds that should post themselves | **RSS** | The automatic pipeline |

## The two that look alike

**Reel** and **D1 Studio** both make a video, and the difference is whether you
are in it.

- **D1 Studio** — you have footage, or you will film some. It cuts what you shot.
- **Reel** — you have no footage at all. It writes a script, voices it and builds
  the video from photos.

## The shortcut most people miss

You often do not need this page. On **Ideas**, a topic card's **✦ Make** asks
which format and then opens the right studio *with the brief already filled in*.
That is fewer decisions and a better starting point.
