## Do it

1. **Create** → the **Post** card → **Article Carousel**.
2. Pick your **Account** at the top. **Nothing else appears until you do** — the
   whole form is hidden behind it, and its palette, language and character load
   as soon as you choose.
3. Choose a **Visual Theme**, or leave it on the account's own.
4. On the **🌐 URL** tab, paste the article's address.

5. Tick **Use images from the article** to download its photographs, so you can
   put a real picture on any slide.
6. Set **Language**, **Slides**, **Character** and **Template** underneath.
7. Press **✦ Generate Carousel** and watch the log.

## Instead of pasting

**📋 Pick from Suggestions** beside the URL box lists the articles Diwche has
already found for your keywords, each with its source. One click fills the field.

## The options

| Option | What to pick |
| --- | --- |
| **Slides** | **Auto** lets the AI decide, which is usually right |
| **Character** | Who writes it. The account's default is preselected |
| **Template** | The design. The account's carousel design is preselected |

## Then

The preview appears as **Preview — N slides**. From there you can rewrite text,
swap pictures, add a video slide, change the theme, and post.

Save at any point with **💾 Save Progress** and pick it up later from the
Activity Log.
