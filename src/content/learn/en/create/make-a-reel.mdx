Reels Studio is for when you have **no footage**. If you filmed something, use
[D1 Studio](/learn/d1/start-a-video/) instead.

<Shot name="reels-source" label="Reels Studio" />

## The steps

**Script → Editor → Voice → Cover → Result.**

An article or prompt reel shows only four — the Voice step is skipped, because
the narration is made with the script. Completed steps stay clickable, so you can
go back.

## Step 1 — Script

1. **Reels** in the left rail, and pick your **Account**.
2. Choose a source: **📰 Article** or **✍ Prompt**.
   - **Article** splits again: **📰 From my articles** lists carousels you have
     already made; **🔗 Paste a link** takes any URL, with **📋 Pick from
     Suggestions** beside it.
   - **Prompt** takes a brief. Be specific — *The Starry Night, focus on the
     brushwork, not his biography.*
3. Set **Duration** — 30s to 4 min, each showing its word estimate.
4. Set **Language** and **Character**.
5. **Generate Script →**.

The script appears in an editable box with a word count. Change it directly, or
**✎ Rewrite with AI**. This is the voice-over, so read it out loud once before
moving on.

Then **Edit in Video Editor →**.

## Step 2 — Editor

Everything scraped for the article is loaded into a video editor **inside this
page**, with the narration already on the voice lane. Build the reel there — see
[the video editor](/learn/studio/open-the-editor/) — then press **Use in Reel**.

Title, caption and theme are set on this step too.

## Step 3 — Voice

Only for footage reels.

- **🤖 AI voice-over** or **🎙 Record my voice**.
- For AI: **Azure** or **ElevenLabs**, then a voice.
- **Music**: **My Library**, **My Uploads** or **None**.
- **Generate Voice Preview →**, then listen to each scene. **🔄 Re-voice** any
  one you do not like.

## Step 4 — Cover

Pick a cover from your Gallery or upload one, or press **✦ Cover from my
template** to render your account's Reel cover design with this reel's title.

Optional — Instagram picks a frame if you skip it.

## Step 5 — Result

Check the bumpers, then **▶ Generate Video**. When it finishes:

- **Download MP4**
- **Post to Instagram**
- **💾 Save as Draft** — into the Activity Log for approval

Title and caption are editable here too, with **↺ Regenerate**.

## Save at any point

**💾 Save Progress** is on every step. Resume from the Activity Log.
