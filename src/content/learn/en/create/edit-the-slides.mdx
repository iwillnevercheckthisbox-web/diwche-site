## Rewrite a slide

1. Press **✎ Edit Text** on the slide.
2. The panel names it — *Edit text for slide N* — and gives you **Headline** and
   **Body**. In Body, **one point per line**.
3. **Save**.

**✨ AI Rewrite** in the same panel rewrites that slide in your character's
voice. Useful when the meaning is right and the phrasing is not.

Some slides are photo-only and have nothing to edit; the panel says so.

## Reorder and remove

On each slide: **◀** and **▶** move it, **🗑** deletes it. The index badge shows
where you are — *3 / 7*.

The first slide is the one people see in the feed. Whatever earns the swipe
belongs there.

## Add a video slide

**🎬 Add Video Slide** at the top. Choose from your Gallery, with **🔇 Mute
audio** if you want it silent — or import from a **YouTube or Instagram
reel/post URL**, with **🔤 Burn in subtitles** if you want captions on it.

If it cannot be framed by the template it is added as a plain page, and it tells
you why.

## Change the whole look

**Switch Theme**, then **↺ Apply Theme**. Every slide is redrawn in the new
palette. The text is untouched.

## The caption

Under the preview. Edit it directly, or **↺ Regenerate Caption**.

## Then post it

- **⬇ Download** — the images
- **📲 Post as Carousel** — straight to Instagram
- **🎵 Post as Reel** — the slides as a video
- **💾 Save as Draft** — into the Activity Log for approval

A draft saved for review expires in 24 hours if nothing touches it.
