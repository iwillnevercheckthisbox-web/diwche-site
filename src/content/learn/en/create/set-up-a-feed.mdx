This is the automatic side of Diwche: it watches news feeds, keeps the articles
that match your keywords, and posts them on a schedule.

<Shot name="rss-page" label="RSS Pipeline" />

## Add a feed

1. **RSS** in the left rail, and pick your account.
2. At the bottom, fill in **Feed name** and **RSS URL (https://…)**, then **+ Add
   Feed**.
3. Repeat for each source.

## Add keywords

Expand the feed. Add keywords with **+ add keyword** → **Add**. Only articles
matching a keyword are ever considered.

Each keyword chip has **two toggles**:

- **AI image for this keyword** — generate a picture
- **Use article's own photo for this keyword** — take the photograph from the
  article

The legend under them shows which is which. Use the article's own photo for news;
generate for subjects where the source photography is poor.

## Write the call to action

**Default Call to Action** is appended to every auto-posted caption. **Save
CTA**.

## Set the schedule

<Shot name="rss-schedule" callouts={[
  { x: 22, y: 55, text: 'Leave this on until you trust it' },
]} />

**Posting Schedule** offers two modes:

- **Manual times** — you add the times. Predictable.
- **Smart (adaptive)** — it works out the best times from your own results.
  **↻ Recompute Now** refreshes them.

Then the checkbox that matters most:

> **Require review before posting to Instagram** — saves as "Pending Review" in
> the Activity Log instead of publishing

**Leave this on until you trust it.** With it on, everything the pipeline makes
waits for you in the Activity Log. Turn it off and posts go out by themselves.

**Save Schedule**.

## Run it and watch

**Run now** does a pass immediately, with a live log. **Pause** stops the
schedule and keeps everything. **Clear history** wipes the record of what has
been posted — which also means articles it has already used can come round
again.

## Nothing gets posted twice

Every article is checked against what this account has already published. That is
the one thing you do not have to manage.
