No link, no file — just what you want to say.

## Do it

1. **Create** → **Post** → **Article Carousel**.
2. Pick your **Account**.
3. Choose the **✏️ Prompt** tab.

<Shot name="carousel-sources" callouts={[
  { x: 56, y: 44, text: 'The Prompt tab' },
]} />

4. Write into **Your Prompt**. Be specific about the angle, not just the subject.
5. Optionally add photos under **📸 Add Photos** — these are used *directly* as
   the carousel's images, not fed to an image generator.
6. Set the options and press **✦ Generate Carousel**.

## Write a good prompt

A subject alone gives you an encyclopedia entry. Say what you want it to argue
and who for:

> Why nobody paints skies the way they did in 1850 — focus on the pigment going
> out of production, not on the painters. For people who like paintings but were
> never taught how to look at one. Five slides.

The parts that do the work: the **angle**, what to **leave out**, the
**audience**, and the **length**.

## The shortcut

On **Ideas**, pressing **✦ Make → Carousel** on a topic card opens this page with
the Prompt tab selected and the brief already written from that topic — its hook,
its pillar and its fact anchor. That is a better prompt than most people write
from a blank box.
