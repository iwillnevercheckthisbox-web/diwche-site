Two requirements, and the message you see tells you which one is missing.

## "Open this page in Chrome or Edge"

The editors use browser features Safari and Firefox do not have. There is no
setting and no workaround — open Diwche in **Chrome or Edge**.

Everything else in Diwche works in any browser. It is only the two editors.

## "Choose your media folder"

The editors keep your media on your own disk, and they need a folder to use.

1. Press the button and pick one.
2. **Avoid Desktop, Documents and Downloads.** The browser treats them specially
   and may refuse.
3. **Avoid anything that syncs** — Dropbox, iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Google Drive.
   A sync client moving files under the editor causes problems that look like
   corruption.

A plain new folder, somewhere you back up, is the right answer.

## It asks again on another computer

Expected. The folder is on one machine, so each computer gets its own. That is
also why a project can say *File not on this device* — see
[Use the Gallery](/learn/library/use-the-gallery/).

## It forgot the folder

Browsers do sometimes drop the permission — after clearing site data, or a long
gap. Choose the same folder again and everything in it comes back.
