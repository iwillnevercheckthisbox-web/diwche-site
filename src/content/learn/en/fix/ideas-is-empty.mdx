Work down this list; it is in order of likelihood.

## 1. No account is picked

> Pick an account — Diwche knows each page separately.

Choose one in the dropdown at the top.

## 2. There is no Direction yet

If the three-step setup opens instead of the tabs, that is the answer. Diwche
cannot suggest anything until it knows who your page is for and what it will
never cover.

It takes about five minutes — see
[Tell Diwche about your page](/learn/page/tell-diwche-about-your-page/).

## 3. Nothing has been generated today

<Shot name="ideas-today" label="Ideas, working" />

> Nothing yet for today.

Press **Ask Diwche**. The first ten topics of the day take about a minute.

## 4. You dismissed them all

Look at the foot of the list for **Show N you said no to**. If that number is
high, they were made — you just said no. Press **10 more**.

## 5. The sources are quiet

A pause notice at the bottom of the page means Instagram reading is paused.
Today's topics still arrive; only the peer material is missing.

## Getting better topics, not just more

If topics keep arriving and none of them are right, the fix is not more topics:

- Use **👎** and **write the note**. *Too generic — I want named people and
  dates* changes tomorrow's list.
- Dismiss with the real reason rather than ignoring.
- Add **accounts you admire**, so it has examples of what good looks like for
  your subject.
- Sharpen **Never** in your Direction. Ruling things out narrows what is left.
