Everything you need is on the row.

## Look at the row

In the **Activity Log**, filter Status to **Failed**. The row shows **⚠** and the
reason, and underneath it what the automatic retry is doing:

- *Auto-retry soon (attempt 2)* — it is going to try again by itself.
- *Auto-retry in ~12m (attempt 3)* — same, later.
- *Auto-retry stopped after N attempts — retry manually below* — it has given up.

## Then

1. **Check the connection first.** Go to the account and look at the Instagram
   badge. A failed post is a disconnected account far more often than anything
   else — see
   [Instagram says it is disconnected](/learn/fix/instagram-says-disconnected/).
2. **↻ Retry** on the row.
3. If it fails again with the same message, read the message. It is Instagram's
   own, and it usually names the problem.

## Common reasons

| What it says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Anything about permissions | Reconnect and accept all of them |
| Anything about the media | The file did not meet Instagram's requirements — usually a video's length or shape |
| Anything about the token | Reconnect |

## Give up on one

**✕ Remove** throws the record away and stops the retries.
