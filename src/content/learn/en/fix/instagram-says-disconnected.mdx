Almost always the same fix. Go to **Accounts** → the account, look at the badge
on the Instagram card, and press **Reconnect Instagram**.

<Shot name="ig-card-connected" label="A healthy connection" />

## By badge

| Badge | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Reconnect soon** | Reconnect before the date on the card. Posting still works until then |
| **Expired** | Reconnect. It ran out and cannot renew itself |
| **Access withdrawn** | Reconnect. The app was removed, the password changed, or Instagram cancelled it |
| **Missing permission** | Reconnect, and **accept every permission** |
| **Not connected** | Connect Instagram |

Reconnecting takes fifteen seconds and changes nothing about the account — same
handle, same history, same schedule.

## Missing permission is the sneaky one

It looks almost identical to **Connected** at a glance, and posting fails while
everything else works. It happens when a permission was switched off on
Instagram's own consent screen. Reconnect and accept all of them.

## If reconnecting does not stick

Check the account is still a **Professional** account — Business or Creator. If
it was switched back to personal, no app can post to it.

## If the card says it is not set up

> Connecting isn't set up on this server yet

Nothing to do with your account. An administrator has to configure it.
