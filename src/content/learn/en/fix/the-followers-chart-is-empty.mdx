Different sections need different things. Find yours.

## "Nothing to show yet" on the whole page

Nothing has been captured. Connect Instagram if it is not, then press **Capture
today** at the top — that takes a reading immediately instead of waiting for
tonight.

## Followers online, by hour

<Shot name="performance-hours" label="When to post" />

> Instagram only returns this once an account has around 100+ followers.

Not your fault and not fixable from here. Under about a hundred followers,
Instagram does not provide it.

It can also mean the **insights permission** is missing. Check with **Test
Instagram** on the account page — a good result ends with *Insights permission: ✓
granted*. If it says missing, reconnect and accept every permission.

Meanwhile, use the chart beside it — **your average reach, by hour published** —
which has no follower minimum and is better evidence anyway.

## Growth is empty

The line is drawn from nightly captures. On day one there is nothing to draw.
**Capture today** starts it.

## Best and worst is empty

It needs posts inside the window you picked. Try **90d** instead of **7d**.

## What's actually working is empty

The strictest one: a group needs **at least 3 posts**, and every post has to have
finished its **14-day** tracking window. A page in its first fortnight has
nothing here, and that is correct rather than broken.

## What worked, and why is empty

A post is read three days after it goes out. Come back once a few posts have had
their three days.

## The consultant report will not generate

It needs one of: a few posts with a full 14-day life, a couple of days of account
insights, or some peer accounts. Adding peers is usually the quickest.
