Five steps. Most of the twenty minutes is the third one, and it is the one that
makes everything afterwards good.

## 1. Connect Instagram

**Accounts** → your account → the **Instagram connection** card at the top →
**Connect Instagram**.

<Shot name="ig-card-not-connected" callouts={[
  { x: 25, y: 60, text: 'Start here' },
]} />

Your Instagram account has to be **Professional** — Business or Creator. Accept
every permission on the screen Instagram shows you.

Full steps: [Connect your Instagram account](/learn/accounts/connect-instagram/).

## 2. Fill in the identity

Same page, the **Account Identity** tab. Four fields are enough for now:

<Shot name="identity-top" callouts={[
  { x: 25, y: 43, text: 'Drawn onto your designs' },
  { x: 25, y: 78, text: 'Colour and type for everything' },
]} />

**Page Name**, **Instagram Handle**, **Default Palette**, **Tone of voice**. Then
**Save Identity**.

The first two get drawn onto every design. Leave the handle blank and that part
of the design comes out empty.

## 3. Tell Diwche about your page

**Ideas** → pick your account → talk for three minutes about who the page is for,
what you will post about, and what it should never be.

This is the step to do properly. Everything Diwche suggests from now on is
written from it, and five careless minutes here produce months of generic
suggestions.

Check **What Diwche heard**, fix anything misheard, then work through the
Direction card and press **Looks right ✓**.

Full steps:
[Tell Diwche about your page](/learn/page/tell-diwche-about-your-page/).

## 4. Make something

Back on **Ideas**, you now have topics.

<Shot name="ideas-today" callouts={[
  { x: 26, y: 49, text: 'Pick one and press Make' },
]} />

Press **✦ Make** on one you like, choose a format, and the right studio opens
with the brief already filled in. Change what you want, then save it.

Nothing you make posts by itself.

## 5. Approve it

**Activity Log**. Your post is there as **Pending Review**.

<Shot name="activity-log" callouts={[
  { x: 12, y: 42, text: 'Look at it before you approve it' },
]} />

Click the thumbnail and look at it properly — that lightbox is the only place you
see it as a reader will. Then **✔ Approve & Post**, or pick a time and press
**📅 Schedule**.

## Then what

- **Come back tomorrow.** Ideas refreshes, and it changes based on what you
  picked today.
- **Use 👍 and 👎** on the topics. The note under a thumbs down is the fastest
  way to make them better.
- **Give it two weeks** before judging Performance. Most of that page needs posts
  that have finished their fourteen-day window.
