A template is the layout. A palette is the colour and type poured into it. Two
separate things, on purpose: change the palette and every template you use
follows.

<Shot name="templates-list" label="Templates" />

## Use one

1. **Templates** in the left rail.
2. Pick one and open it with **Use**.
3. Set three things:
   - **Palette** — leave it on the account's own unless this design needs its own.
   - **How much is written** — how dense the text should be.
   - **Show or hide** — which elements appear: the page name, the handle, the
     slogan, the ornament.
4. Save it. It now appears under your own designs.

## Make it the default

**Accounts → your account → Account Identity → Designs**, and pick it in the row
for its kind — Post, Carousel, Story, Reel cover or Reel frame.

A template belongs to one kind. A carousel design will not appear in the Story
row.

## Designing one from scratch

Building a template is an operator job — it needs the `operator` role, and it
opens a different editor. If you have it, **Templates** → open a template's own
editor rather than **Use**.
