## Upload one

1. **Fonts** in the left rail, under Library.
2. Choose the **File**.
3. Give it a **Name** — this is what appears in every font dropdown, so name it
   the way you think of it.
4. Save.

## Where it becomes available

- **Palettes** → **Typography**, as the display or body face
- **Video editor** → the **🔤 Text** tab
- **Subtitle Studio** → **Caption Style**, and its random-font list
- The photo editor's **Text** tool

## For Persian and Arabic

Check the font actually has the characters. A Latin typeface will accept Persian
text and render boxes, or silently substitute another face — which looks like a
bug and is really a missing glyph set.

## Two is enough

A display face and a body face. The random-caption feature needs at least two
fonts to vary between, which is another reason to add a second, but a page with
six typefaces looks like six pages.

## Rights

Fonts are licensed. Check yours allows use in published images before you build a
brand on it.
