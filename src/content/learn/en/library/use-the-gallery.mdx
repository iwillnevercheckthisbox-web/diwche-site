The Gallery is everything you have saved — photos you designed, images you
downloaded, video you kept.

<Shot name="pe-publish" label="Save to Gallery" />

## Use it

**Gallery** in the left rail, under Library.

Each item has **✎ Edit**, which opens it in the photo editor, and **🗑 Delete**.

## Where it feeds

The Gallery is the shared shelf every studio reaches into:

- **Video editor** → **🖼 Upload from Gallery**
- **Article Carousel** → the video-slide picker
- **D1 Studio** → **from your Gallery**
- **Reels Studio** → the cover picker
- **Stories** → the overlay picture

Saving to the Gallery is what makes a picture available everywhere else. It is
worth doing even when you have downloaded the file.

## "File not on this device"

You will meet this if you use more than one computer.

The editors keep your media in a folder on **your own disk**, and that folder is
on one machine. Open a project on a different computer and the pictures it points
at are not there.

What to do:

- Go back to the computer that has the folder, or
- Re-upload the file on the machine you are using now.

Nothing is lost. The item is still listed; this machine just cannot see the file.

## Keep the folder

The folder you chose is the **only copy** of anything that was never uploaded to
the server. Do not point it at a temporary directory, and do back it up.
