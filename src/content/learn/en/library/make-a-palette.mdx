A palette is colour **and type** together. Everything Diwche draws for an
account — carousels, stories, reel frames, covers — is coloured by it.

<Shot name="palettes-list" label="Palettes" />

## Make one

**Palettes** in the left rail → make a new one. The editor has five sections.

### Colours

Four you set, and two derived from them. Set the four:

- A **background**
- An **ink** for text on that background
- An **accent** for the one thing that should be noticed
- A **secondary**

The derived two are worked out for you, so a design always has a mid-tone
available.

### Derived

Shows what was computed. Look at it — this is where you find that your accent has
become invisible on your background.

### Typography

A **display** face for headlines and a **body** face for everything else. These
are also what caption styling pins to the top of its font list.

### Art direction

Prose describing the look — the mood, what the imagery should be, what to avoid.
This is sent along when pictures are generated, so it is worth more than it
looks.

### Preview

The palette on a real design. Check here before saving.

## Watch the contrast warnings

The editor warns when a pair does not have enough contrast. Take them seriously:
Instagram is read on a phone, outdoors, at arm's length. Ink that is nearly the
background colour reads as a blank slide.

## Use it

**Accounts → your account → Account Identity → Default Palette.** Everything that
account makes from then on uses it. Change the palette and every *future* design
changes with it.
