## Two tabs

**Curated Library** — what ships with Diwche, organised by mood.

**My Uploads** — yours.

## Upload one

1. **Music** in the left rail, under Library.
2. **My Uploads**, then choose a **File**.
3. Give it a **Name** you will recognise later.
4. Add a **Mood tag** — this is what the studios filter by, so it matters more
   than the name.
5. Save.

## Tag it usefully

The mood tag is how a track gets found six weeks later. Tag by the *feeling of
the video it suits*, not by genre — *calm*, *driving*, *hopeful*, *tense* —
because that is the question you will be asking when you pick one.

## Where it turns up

- **Video editor** → Inspector → **🎵 Audio** → **Pick music…**
- **Reels Studio** → the Voice step → **Music** → **My Uploads**
- **D1 Studio** uses the account's music by itself

## Rights

Anything you upload plays on your posts. Use music you are allowed to use —
Instagram mutes or takes down what you are not.
