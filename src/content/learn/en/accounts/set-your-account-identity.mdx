This is the page that decides what everything Diwche makes for you looks and
sounds like. Fill it in once.

## Open it

**Accounts** → your account → the **Account Identity** tab.

## The fields, in order

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | What you call this account inside Diwche. Appears in every dropdown |
| **Page Name** | Drawn onto carousel and infographic slides. Any language |
| **Instagram Handle** | Drawn on designs as @handle. Leave off the @ |
| **Slogan** | A short line about the page, for templates that have a slot for one |
| **Default Palette** | Colour **and type** for everything this account makes |
| **Tone of voice** | How every script, caption and title is written |
| **Hashtags in the first comment** | Moves them out of the caption |
| **Start / End Bumper** | Short branded clips cut into generated reels |
| **Designs** | One design per kind of content — [its own article](/learn/accounts/choose-your-designs/) |
| **Strategy plan** | How many weekly ideas, and their mix |
| **Content Language** | The language of RSS captions and banners |

Then **Save Identity**.

## The three that get drawn on things

Page Name, Handle and Slogan are printed onto designs. Leave the handle blank and
that box on the template renders as nothing — no error, just a gap. So if a
design looks oddly empty, this is usually why.

## Tone of voice

Six choices, and they rewrite every generated word:

- **Friendly** — a knowledgeable friend explaining it. The default
- **Casual** — how people actually speak
- **Funny** — dry and observational, never forced
- **Bold** — short, certain, no hedging
- **Warm** — personal, first person, unhurried
- **Formal** — precise and impersonal

## Hashtags in the first comment

Off by default. Turn it on and hashtags go into comment #1 instead of the
caption, which buys back the two lines Instagram shows before *more*. Instagram
treats them the same either way.

## Bumpers

**Start Bumper** is a short branded clip cut into generated reels; **End Bumper**
goes on the end. Each has an **Enabled** checkbox and, for the start one, an
**Insert Second** — generated reels snap to the nearest scene cut near that
point.

## Strategy plan

Four numbers: **Ideas / week**, and the mix between **Own %** (your own proven
themes), **Peers %** (breakouts from accounts like yours) and **Fresh %** (news).
Leave Ideas/week on *auto* unless you have a reason.

## Content Language

Persian, English or German. This one is about **content**, not the interface —
it decides what language RSS captions and banners are written in, and it also
decides which way the text runs on your designs.
