Nothing publishes until this is done. Diwche can find topics, write captions and
build every design without it — it just has nowhere to send them.

## Before you start

Your Instagram account has to be a **Professional** account, Business or Creator.
On your phone: Instagram → Settings → **Account type and tools**. Switching is
free, takes a few taps, and changes nothing about your handle, posts or
followers.

You do not need a Facebook account or a Facebook Page.

## Connect it

1. Open **Accounts** in the left rail and click the account you want to connect.

2. At the top of the page, above the tabs, find the **Instagram connection**
   card.

   <Shot name="ig-card-not-connected" callouts={[
     { x: 83, y: 14, text: 'The badge — this is where a problem shows up first' },
     { x: 25, y: 60, text: 'Click here' },
   ]} />

3. Click **Connect Instagram**. The button changes to *Opening Facebook…* and
   the whole page navigates away to Instagram. It is a full-page redirect, not a
   pop-up — you have not lost anything.

4. Sign in, then read the permissions screen and **accept all of them**. You can
   switch individual permissions off, and a connection missing one looks
   completely fine until the thing it covers stops working.

5. Instagram sends you back to the same page, and a message says *Instagram
   connected.*

     { x: 84, y: 10, text: 'Connected' },
     { x: 33, y: 20, text: 'The account it will post as' },
     { x: 36, y: 29, text: 'When you next need to reconnect' },
     { x: 36, y: 58, text: 'The permissions Instagram granted' },
   ]} />

That is it. The account can publish now.

## Read the badge

The badge in the corner of the card is how Diwche tells you something needs
doing. It is checked on a schedule, so a problem usually appears here days
before a post would have failed.

| Badge | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | Nothing |
| **Reconnect soon** | Click **Reconnect Instagram** before the date on the card |
| **Expired** | Click **Reconnect Instagram** |
| **Access withdrawn** | Click **Reconnect Instagram** |
| **Missing permission** | Click **Reconnect Instagram** and accept every permission |
| **Not connected** | Click **Connect Instagram** |

The accounts list says it more bluntly — an account that needs you carries a
**⚠ Reconnect needed** link straight to its page.

## Reconnect, twice a year

A connected card usually shows a line reading *Reauthorisation due by* and a
date. Click **Reconnect Instagram** before it, sign in, accept the same
permissions. Fifteen seconds, and nothing about the account changes — same
handle, same history, same schedule.

Leave it and the badge turns to **Reconnect soon**, then **Expired**, and posts
stop going out.

## Get comments faster

If comment auto-reply is on, the card says how comments reach Diwche:

- *Comments arrive instantly* — nothing to do.
- *Comments are still polled every half hour* — click **switch to instant
  delivery** next to it. One second, no downside.

## Check a connection

**Test Instagram**, top right of the account page, asks Instagram who Diwche is
talking to. It posts nothing.

A good result names the handle, lists the permissions, and calls out the one
that is easiest to lose:

> Insights permission: ✓ granted

If that says **missing**, posting works but Performance will have no numbers in
it. Reconnect and accept everything.

## Disconnect

**Disconnect** on the card stops the account posting until it is connected
again. Nothing else goes — feeds, keywords, palettes, characters, schedule,
identity and the whole Activity Log stay exactly as they are.

## If it will not connect

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Connecting isn't set up on this server yet* | Nothing to do with your account — ask an administrator |
| *No Instagram business account was found* | The account is still personal, or not linked to a Page you manage |
| *Instagram did not identify the account* | Switch it to Professional in the Instagram app, then try again |
| *That connection attempt has expired* | Go back to the account and click **Connect Instagram** again |
| It says connected, but posts fail | Look at the badge — **Missing permission** looks almost identical to **Connected** |
