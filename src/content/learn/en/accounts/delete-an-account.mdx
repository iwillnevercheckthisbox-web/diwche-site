## Before you do

If you only want it to stop posting, you do not need this. **Disconnect** on the
Instagram card stops publishing and keeps everything. Pausing the pipeline on the
RSS page stops the automatic runs.

Delete when the page is genuinely finished with.

## Do it

1. **Accounts** → the account → **Account Identity**.
2. Scroll to the bottom, to **Delete this account**.
3. Read what it lists, then confirm.

## What goes

<Shot name="identity-top" label="Account Identity" />

Permanently, and it cannot be undone:

- The account itself, and its Instagram connection
- Its posted history and stories
- Its feeds and keywords
- Its scraped posts and suggestions
- Its YouTube pools
- Its saved ideas and its Direction

## What stays

Everything shared across your accounts:

- **Designs and templates**
- **Fonts**
- **Palettes**, **Characters**, **Music** and the **Gallery**

## What it does not touch

**Your Instagram page.** Deleting the account here removes Diwche's copy of
things and its access. Your posts, followers and profile are untouched — nothing
is deleted on Instagram.
