Five kinds of content, five dropdowns. Whatever you pick here is what Diwche
uses every time it makes that kind of thing.

## Where

**Accounts** → your account → **Account Identity** → the **Designs** card.

## The five

| Row | Used by |
| --- | --- |
| **Post / RSS card** | Single posts, and the cards made from RSS articles |
| **Carousel** | Article, prompt and photo carousels |
| **Story** | The story digest, and Story Studio |
| **Reel cover** | Reels Studio's *Cover from template* |
| **Reel frame** | Burned over every reel |

Each starts on a built-in, shown as *…(built-in default)*. The thumbnail on the
left updates when you change the dropdown, so you can see what you picked.

## Use one of your own

1. Go to **Templates** in the left rail and design or save one.
2. Come back here. It is now in the dropdown for its kind.

A template belongs to one kind — a carousel design does not appear in the Story
row.

## Reel frame has an extra option

Only that row has **Off — no frame on reposted Reels**. It turns the frame off
for reposts from the scraper, and leaves it on everything else.

## Change it whenever

It applies to what you make *next*. Nothing already posted changes.
