Diwche runs any number of accounts side by side. Each is its own page with its
own everything, and work on one never touches another.

## Add one

**Accounts** → **+ New account**. Give it a name, then work through
[Account Identity](/learn/accounts/set-your-account-identity/) and
[connect Instagram](/learn/accounts/connect-instagram/) as you did the first.

## Switch between them

Most pages — Ideas, Performance, RSS, Stories, the studios — have an account
dropdown at the top right. Change it and the whole page follows. The Activity Log
has **All Accounts** as well, so you can see everything at once.

## What is separate

Almost everything: feeds and keywords, the Direction and its pillars, topics,
schedule, designs, tone of voice, content language, the Instagram connection,
and the whole posted history.

A failure on one account never stops another.

## What is shared

The things you build once and reuse: **Palettes**, **Templates**, **Characters**,
**Fonts**, **Music** and the **Gallery** all belong to you, not to one account.

That is why deleting an account leaves them alone.

## Reading the list

Each row shows the account, its progress ring, a seven-day ribbon, a follower
sparkline and its vitals. An account that needs you carries **⚠ Reconnect
needed**, which links straight to its page.
