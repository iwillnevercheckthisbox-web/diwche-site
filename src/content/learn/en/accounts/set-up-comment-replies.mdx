With this on, new comments on your posts get answered automatically, in the voice
of a character you pick.

## Do it

1. **Accounts** → your account → **Account Identity**, and scroll to **💬 Comment
   Auto-Reply**.
2. Tick **Enable auto-reply to comments**.
3. Under **Who answers**, pick a character. A line of description appears under
   the picker.
4. Type a comment into **Preview a reply** — write it the way a follower would —
   and press **Preview reply**.
5. If you like the answer, **Save Identity**. If not, try another character and
   preview again.

The preview posts nothing to Instagram, and works whether or not you have saved.

## Who answers

The same characters that write everything else — Hadi (sarcastic and sharp),
Behta (warm and friendly), Saba (calm and analytical), Farnaz (artistic), Lena
(news analyst) and the rest, plus any you have made your own. Edit them on the
**Characters** page.

For replies, warm usually beats sharp: a comment is a person, not an audience.

## What it leaves alone

Spam and hateful comments are not answered.

## Make replies arrive faster

Comments reach Diwche either instantly or on a half-hour timer, and the
Instagram connection card at the top of the page says which. If it says polled
every half hour, click **switch to instant delivery** — see [Connect your
Instagram account](/learn/accounts/connect-instagram/).
