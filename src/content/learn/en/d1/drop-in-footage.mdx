Three ways, on the **⬚ Yes — I have the footage** door:

- **Drag** clips and photos onto the zone. Any order.
- **Click** the zone to choose files.
- **from your Gallery** to use what is already in Diwche.

### The Photos-app trap

> Dragging a video out of the Photos app hands over a still frame, not the video.

This catches nearly everyone on a Mac. If you drag from Photos you get a
photograph of your video. **Click to choose and pick Photos in the sidebar**, or
drag from Finder instead. If nothing you added is a video, a warning says so.

## The meter

Under the tiles, something like **12 min of 20 · 1.4 GB**. The limits:

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| One file | 500 MB |
| One clip | 10 minutes |
| Everything together | 20 minutes |
| How many things | 60 |

A clip over ten minutes is removed from the pile and it tells you which. Trim it
first and add it again.

## Tell it what it is looking at

Three fields, and the last two are what separate a good cut from a generic one:

- **Language**
- **Where / what is this?** — *e.g. Vienna, Karlsplatz, Saturday morning*
- **Anything you want said about it?** — *optional. What to bring out, what to
  leave alone, how it should feel.* This **overrides anything we would have
  decided ourselves**, so it is the strongest instruction you can give.

  > e.g. keep it warm, don't make it sound like an advert

Both boxes have a **🎙** if you would rather say it.

## Start it

**Make something →**. Then:

> Stay on this page until it hands over — about a minute. Closing the tab would
> drop the files.

Once the upload is done you can go anywhere; it tells you when it is ready.
