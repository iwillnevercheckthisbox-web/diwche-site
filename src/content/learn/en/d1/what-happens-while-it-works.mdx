Once you press **Make something →** or **Make it →**, D1 goes away and works.
The screen you are on is worth reading rather than staring at.

## The contact sheet

<Shot name="d1-footage" label="What you gave it" />

Your clips are shown as tiles, and each gets a badge as D1 decides what it is:

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **🎙** | You talking. This is the spine of the video |
| **🎬** | Something to look at. A cutaway over the talking |
| **✕** | Not used |

Watching those appear tells you whether it understood the material. If your best
clip is marked ✕, you will be able to change that on the review screen.

## The line underneath

One sentence, from the worker itself, updating as it goes — starting at
*Looking at what you sent…* or *Listening to your take…*. If the job is queued
behind another, a second line says so.

It ends at **Ready — have a look.** or, if it went wrong, at a line saying what
failed with **← Try again** beside it.

## You can close the page

> You can close this page — you'll be told when it's ready.

That is true from the moment the upload finishes. A toast confirms the handover —
*Cutting your video — we'll tell you when it's ready* — and the bell tells you
when it is done. **Watch it happen** stays if you would rather look.

## The three buttons

- **See it →** — once it has finished, to the review screen.
- **← Try again** — after a failure.
- **Start over** — back to the first question, always.
