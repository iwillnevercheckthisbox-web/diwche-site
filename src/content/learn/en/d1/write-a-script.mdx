## Say what it is about

1. On the **✎ Not yet — I have an idea** door, type into the box. One line is
   enough — the script is written from your page's Direction, so it already knows
   your audience, your voice and what you never cover.

   > e.g. why the old water pipes under this city still work

2. Or press the **🎙** in the corner of the box and say it. It writes down what
   you said.
3. Set the **Language**.
4. **Or something Diwche suggested** — a dropdown of today's topics. Pick one and
   the subject fills in.
5. **Write me a script →**.

It needs at least four characters, and it takes a few seconds.

## Check the script

The next screen is **Say it like this?**

> Change any of it — you're the one reading it out loud.

It comes in parts:

- **The first line — this is the one that matters.** The hook. It is labelled
  that way because it is the one that decides whether anyone watches the rest.
- **Then** — one field per beat.
- **And finally** — the sign-off.

A chip says **about Ns to say**, and turns red if you are well over the length
you asked for.

## Make it yours

Read it out loud before you continue. Two things to fix almost every time:

- **Sentences you would not say.** If you stumble reading it, the camera will
  catch you stumbling.
- **The first line.** Make it a fact or a question, not a preamble.

Then **Ready to record →**.
