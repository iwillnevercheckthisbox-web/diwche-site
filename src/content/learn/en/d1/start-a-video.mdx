D1 Studio takes what you filmed — or what you are about to film — and cuts it,
captions it and puts it together.

It opens with one question.

## Have you filmed it yet?

**✎ Not yet — I have an idea**

> We write the script, you read it off the screen while we record you.

Take this one when you know what you want to say and you are willing to be on
camera. It writes the script, puts it on a teleprompter, and records you reading
it.

**⬚ Yes — I have the footage**

> Drop the clips and photos you shot. We listen to them and cut them together.

Take this one when you already shot something. It listens to what you said,
works out which clips are you talking and which are things to look at, and
assembles them.

## Either way

Both doors end the same: you get a finished cut with captions, your account's
frame and music, on a review screen where you can change any of it.

## What you can change before it starts

The **Advanced** fold, on both doors:

- **How long?** — **Auto** reads it off the footage, or fix it at 30 seconds to
  4 minutes.
- **Will you be on camera?** — Yes, my voice only, or neither. Idea door only.
- **What should we make?** — *My video, with a few cutaways*, *Just my video,
  nothing over it*, or *My voice over pictures*.

Left alone, everything comes from the account: its language, its caption style,
its frame, its music.
