The review screen is the video on the left and a column of cards on the right.
Work down the cards.

The header tells you what it did: **N seconds of recording became M**, plus how
many bits were removed.

<Shot name="d1-ask" label="Where it started" />

## The voice

Three choices: **Yours**, **Write me a script**, **No voice**.

*Yours* keeps what you said. *No voice* strips it and lets the pictures carry it.
*Write me a script* writes narration that fits what is on screen, then reads it —
choose **What to say** and **Read by**, and press **Read it for me**.

If nobody spoke in the footage, it says so.

## Subtitles

**On** or **Off**, and a **Language**.

**Fix the words** opens a fold with one line per caption and a timecode. Correct
anything misheard and press **Fix the captions** — it only lights up once you
have changed something.

## What we took out

Every removed piece, with a reason: **pause**, **filler**, **said again**,
**restarted**, or **for length**.

Read this. It is the fastest way to see whether it cut something you wanted —
and if it did, the fix is to open it in the editor and put it back.

If your take was already tight it says *Nothing — your take was already tight.*

## Things to know

Decisions it had to make on your behalf. Only appears when there were any, and
worth a look.

## What we used

Only on the footage door: every clip you gave it and what it became — **you
talking**, **cutaway**, **on screen** or **not used** — with the reason.

## What D1 heard and saw

The card that lets you argue with it.

**Show it** opens one block per clip: what it heard, a description of what the
picture looked like, and a dropdown for the clip's job — **you talking** /
**something to look at** / **leave it out**.

Two things you can change here:

- **The transcript.** Fix a misheard word. Keep **one word per word** so each
  keeps its timing — it warns you if the count no longer matches.
- **The job.** Promote a clip it skipped, or demote one it leaned on.

On the footage door you can also set the **First line on screen** and the
**Closing line**.

Then:

- **Re-cut with these changes** — only enabled once something has changed
- **Cut it again as it is** — a fresh cut from the same material

A re-cut takes about as long as a render. **Nothing is listened to again**, so it
is cheap to try.

There is also a checkbox to have the reader look at the pile again with your
corrected words — one model call, worth it when you fixed a name that appears
throughout.

## Happy with it?

Write a **Caption**, then:

- **Use this video** — into the Activity Log for approval, like everything else
- **Open in the editor** — the same edit, with every cut, caption and card as a
  normal layer you can change
