Useful when one section of a video should look different from the rest — a
quoted passage, a warning, a punchline.

<Shot name="ve-subtitles-tab" label="The cue table" />

## Do it

1. Tick the checkbox at the start of each cue you want. The header shows **N
   selected**.
2. A panel appears: *Apply to the N selected cue(s) — leaves every other cue
   untouched.*
3. Set a **Text colour**, a **Background** or a **Font size** and press the
   **Apply** beside it.

Each row applies on its own, so you can change the colour without touching the
size.

## Background needs switching on

Setting a **Background** here does nothing visible until **Background box** is
ticked in **Caption Style**. The panel says so. That catches people out: the
value is stored, it is just not being drawn.

## Undo it

**✕ Clear overrides on selected** puts the ticked cues back to the shared style.

**Clear selection** just unticks them without changing anything.
