## Burn them in

**Burn In** draws the captions permanently into a new video file. Instagram has
no subtitle track, so **this is what you want for anything you post** — it is
the only way the captions appear.

1. Press **Burn In**. It changes to **Rendering…**
2. Wait. A panel says *Burning in captions…*
3. When it finishes, **Open rendered video ↗**.

Everything you set — the styling, the per-word colours, the random positions,
the caption area — is in that file exactly as the preview showed it.

## Or download a file

Three buttons beside it:

| Format | What it is | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SRT** | Plain text, the universal one | YouTube, most players |
| **VTT** | Plain text, for the web | A `<video>` on a website |
| **ASS** | Styled — fonts, colours, positions | Handing the exact look to another editor |

SRT and VTT carry the words and the timings and **none of the styling**. ASS
carries the styling too, because it is the format the burn-in itself uses.

## From the video editor instead

If your captions are on a timeline, there is no separate burn-in — they are part
of the video and come out of the normal **▶ Export**. The **Download:** row
there still offers **SRT**, **VTT** and **ASS** if you want the file as well.

## Start again

**Start over** under the export card clears the file and the transcript and
takes you back to the beginning.
