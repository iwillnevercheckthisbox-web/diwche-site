The same transcript can be shown four ways. Changing between them re-cuts the
cues; it does not listen again, and it does not lose your corrections.

<Shot name="ve-subtitles-tab" label="Inspector · Subtitles" />

## Do it

In the **Cue grouping** panel, pick a **Grouping**:

| Grouping | What you get | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One word at a time** | A single word on screen at once | Fast, punchy reels |
| **Fixed word count** | Two to five words per cue, set by a slider | Most short-form video |
| **Line** | A line at a time. The default | Talking head, explainers |
| **Sentence** | A whole sentence | Slow, considered pieces |

With **Fixed word count** a **Words per cue** slider appears, from 2 to 5. Three
is the usual answer.

## It keeps your fixes

The panel says it plainly: *Cuts the transcript into cues again. Text you have
fixed is kept.*

So the sensible order is: transcribe → fix the words → then try the groupings
until one looks right. You are not choosing to redo your corrections.

## What it needs

Word-level timing. If you turned **Word-level timestamps** off when
transcribing, one word at a time has nothing to work from. Leave it on.
