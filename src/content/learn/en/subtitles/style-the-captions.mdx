Everything here is in the **Caption Style** panel, and it applies to every cue
that has no override of its own.

## The basics

- **Font** and **Size** (24–140px).
- **Bold**, **Italic**, **ALL CAPS**.
- **Fill colour** and **Outline** colour, with an **Outline width** (0–10) and
  **Shadow** (0–10).
- **Letter spacing**, −5 to 30.
- **Background box** — a filled panel behind the words.

An outline of 3–4 in a colour opposite the fill is what makes captions readable
over any footage. Shadow alone is not enough on bright backgrounds.

## Let it vary

Three dice buttons make captions look hand-made instead of stamped out:

- **🎲 Random font (per cue)** — each cue picks from a list you build. **Add at
  least two fonts** or nothing varies. Your account's Primary and Secondary
  fonts are pinned to the top of the list.
- **🎲 Random size (per cue)** — a **Size range** with two sliders instead of one
  fixed size.
- **🎲 Random position (per cue)** — a numpad grid of nine positions. **Pick at
  least two** or the captions stay put.

Two positions and two fonts is enough. Nine positions and six fonts reads as a
fault.

## Where they sit

Without random position, one **Position** on the 1–9 grid. **Margins (V / L·R)**
keeps them off the edges — worth setting, since Instagram's own interface covers
the bottom of the screen.

## Highlight the spoken word

**Highlight** has four settings:

- **None**
- **Karaoke fill** — the colour sweeps across each word as it is said
- **Active word colour** — the current word changes colour
- **Pop scale** — the current word grows slightly

**Active word colour** is the one that survives being watched twice. Karaoke
fill is for music.

## Fade

**Fade in / out (ms)** softens the appearance. 80–150ms is enough to stop the
flicker; more and the captions lag the speech.

## Keep them inside a box

**📐 Define area** on the preview lets you drag a rectangle, and every caption is
placed inside it instead of the whole screen. Use it to keep captions clear of a
face, or above the Instagram interface. **📐 Edit area** moves it later, **✕
Clear area** removes it.
