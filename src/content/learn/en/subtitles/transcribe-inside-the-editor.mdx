The video editor has the whole of Subtitle Studio inside it, on the **💬
Subtitles** tab of the Inspector. Captions made here land on the Subtitles lane
and follow the clip they came from.

## Do it

1. **Select a clip on Video 1, or an audio item.** This is the step people miss —
   the button is greyed out until something is selected, because it needs to know
   what to listen to.
2. Open **💬 Subtitles** in the Inspector.
3. Set the Whisper options if you need to.
4. Press **🎙 Transcribe selected clip with Whisper** — or **selected audio**, if
   you picked an audio item.

A **Subtitles** lane appears on the timeline with one block per cue.

## Why it is per clip

Cues are tied to the clip they were transcribed from. Move that clip, and its
captions move with it. Trim it, and they trim. That is why you transcribe a
clip rather than the whole timeline — the captions survive re-editing.

## Add one by hand

**+ Cue at playhead** makes an empty cue where the playhead is. Useful for a
line nobody said out loud.

## Start again

**✕ Clear subtitle track** removes every cue. The clips are untouched.

## No separate burn-in here

In Subtitle Studio there is a **Burn In** button. In the editor there is not,
and does not need to be: the captions are part of the timeline, so they come out
of the normal **▶ Export**.
