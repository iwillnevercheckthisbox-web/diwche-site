The cue table is the list under the preview, headed **Cues (N)**. One row per
caption.

<Shot name="ve-subtitles-tab" label="Cues" />

## Fix a word

Click into the text of a row and type. That is all — there is no save button,
and the preview updates as you type.

## Fix the timing

The two number boxes at the start of each row are the **start** and **end**, in
seconds, stepping by 0.1. Type or use the arrows.

If a caption is flashing past, give it a later end. If it is landing before the
words, give it a later start. A caption should appear a shade *before* the word
is spoken, never after.

## Remove one

The **✕** at the end of the row.

## Add one

**+ Cue at playhead** in the editor, or a cue's own start and end in Subtitle
Studio.

## Your edits survive a regroup

This is the part worth trusting: if you fix the words and then change how the
transcript is cut into cues, **your corrections are kept**. The grouping panel
says so — *Text you have fixed is kept.* So you can correct spelling first and
still change your mind about one word at a time versus whole lines later.
