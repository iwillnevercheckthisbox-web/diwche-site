This is the thing that makes captions look designed rather than generated: one
word in the accent colour, the rest plain.

<Shot name="ve-caption-style" label="Caption Style" />

## Do it

1. Click into a cue's text. The words appear underneath as chips.
2. **Click a word chip.** To style several at once, **Shift-click** the others.
3. The panel names how many you picked — *2 words:* — and offers **Colour**,
   **Font**, **Size**, **Bold** and **Italic**.
4. Set what you want. The preview updates immediately.

## Put it back

**✕ Clear** returns the selected words to the cue's own style. It does not
delete the words.

## What it is for

Two uses, both worth doing:

- **The one word the sentence is about.** "It cost more than **gold**."
- **A number.** Numbers read badly in flowing captions and well when they are
  bigger than everything around them.

Do it to one word in three or four cues, not to every cue. If every word is
emphasised, none is.

## This is not the same as Highlight

**Highlight** in Caption Style colours the word being *spoken*, automatically,
as the video plays. That is a moving effect across every cue. This is a fixed
choice about one particular word.
