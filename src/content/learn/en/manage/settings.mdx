Three sections.

## Profile

Who you are signed in as, and the details attached to your account.

## Devices

The devices signed in to Diwche. Worth a look occasionally; sign out anything you
do not recognise or no longer use.

## Storage

The **media folder** the editors use — the folder on this computer where your
photos and footage are kept.

Two things to know:

**This folder is the only copy.** Anything that was never uploaded to the server
exists only here. If you empty it, or point it at a temporary directory, that
material is gone.

**It is per computer.** Sign in on another machine and you choose a folder there
too. That is why a project opened elsewhere can say *File not on this device* —
see [Use the Gallery](/learn/library/use-the-gallery/).

If you need to move it, choose a new folder here and copy the contents across
yourself.
