## Find something

Four filters across the top: **Account** (or *All Accounts*), **Status**, and a
**from** and **to** date.

The statuses: **Pending Review**, **In Progress**, **Posted**, **Failed**,
**Skipped**, **Rejected**.

## Look at it first

Click the thumbnail. A lightbox opens — video plays, carousels get **‹** and
**›** with a counter, and the caption is underneath. Click the background to
close.

Do this before approving. It is the only place you see the post as a reader will.

## Approve a pending post

The button names what it will do: **✔ Approve & Post**, **✔ Approve & Post
Carousel**, or **✔ Approve & Post Reel**. It asks to confirm, then publishes
immediately.

## Change it first

On a pending row, before you approve:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **✎ Title/Caption** | Edit both. Saving **redraws the banner** with the new title |
| **🖌 Edit image** | Retouch the picture in the photo editor |
| **🖼 Cover** | Pick the Reels-tab cover. Reads *Cover set* afterwards |
| **✂ Final touch** | Re-cut a YouTube-pool reel in the editor |
| **⬇ Download** | Take the file without posting |

## Schedule instead of posting

Pick a date and time in the box, then **📅 Schedule**. The row then shows **⏰**
and the time, and publishes itself. **📅 Reschedule** changes it; **✕ Cancel
schedule** puts it back to manual.

## Say no

**✕ Reject** discards it. It will not be re-queued.

## When something failed

A failed row shows **⚠** and the reason, plus what the automatic retry is doing —
*Auto-retry in ~5m (attempt 2)*, or *Auto-retry stopped after N attempts*.

**↻ Retry** tries again now. **✕ Remove** throws the record away.

## Half-finished work

**In Progress** rows are drafts you left in a studio. **▶ Resume** takes you back
to the right one — Reels Studio, Article Carousel or Data Infographic — exactly
where you stopped. **✕ Discard** throws it away.

## Video projects

A second table at the bottom, **Video Editor Projects**, lists your editor
projects with where they are saved and what is in them. **▶ Resume** opens one.
