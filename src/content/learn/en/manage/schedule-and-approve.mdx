There are two questions: **when**, and **whether you look first**.

<Shot name="rss-schedule" label="Posting Schedule" />

## Whether you look first

Set per account, on the **RSS** page, under Posting Schedule:

> **Require review before posting to Instagram** — saves as "Pending Review" in
> the Activity Log instead of publishing

On, and everything the pipeline makes waits for you. Off, and it publishes by
itself.

**Leave it on for the first few weeks.** You will change your keywords and your
tone once you have seen what comes out, and it is much easier to do that from
rejected drafts than from published posts.

Anything you make by hand in a studio always goes to Pending Review, whatever
this is set to.

## When

Two modes, same page:

- **Manual times** — you add them. Predictable, and right when you know your
  audience.
- **Smart (adaptive)** — worked out from your own results. **↻ Recompute Now**
  refreshes them.

Cross-check either against **Performance → When to post**, which shows both when
your followers are around and when your posts actually did well. Trust the
second.

## One post at a time

A configured time posts **one** thing. Four times a day is four posts, spread
out — never a batch.

## Scheduling one post

You do not need a schedule to time a single post. In the Activity Log, pick a
date and time on the row and press **📅 Schedule**. It publishes itself and you
do not have to come back.

## Nothing goes twice

Every article is checked against what the account has already published. That is
automatic and you do not manage it — unless you press **Clear history** on the
RSS page, which forgets what has been posted and lets old articles come round
again.
