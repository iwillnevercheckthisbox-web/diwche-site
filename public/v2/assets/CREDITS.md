copyright (EU Directive 2019/790 art. 14; Bridgeman v. Corel in the US). The files were taken from the
example folder of the MIT-licensed KwaiVGI/LivePortrait repository, cropped to 4:5 and resized.

No living person appears anywhere on the page. The reels were made here with ffmpeg: a slow camera
move over the painting, graded with one of the app's looks.
