Plate I · The whole

A post, under the glass.

Everything you publish is a body made of smaller parts. Diwche builds it from the inside out. Scroll to take it apart.

Plate II · Ideas

Every post starts as a cell.

Each morning Diwche grows a handful of ideas from what your corner of the internet is talking about. Each one has a nucleus: the reason it should work for your page.

Plate III · Script

The idea becomes code.

The script is the DNA: a hook, a body, an ask. Every format is expressed from these same lines, so nothing drifts from what you meant.

Plate IV · Formats

One code, many organs.

The same script grows into a post, a carousel, a story, a reel and a voiceover. Each layer is built for the way it will be seen.

Plate V · Channels

Then it goes out into the world.

Each format is routed to where your people are, at its own hour: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Telegram, your blog and X.

Follow one idea

From a voice note to six channels, in the app you already hold.

01 Onboarding

Tell it about your page, out loud.

Talk for a minute about what you post and who for. Diwche turns it into a brief: your niche, your voice, your people, how often you want to post.

Voice or textEnglish, Deutsch, فارسی
02 Ideas

Every morning, ideas that fit your page.

It reads what your corner of the internet is talking about today and hands you a few ideas, each with the hook and the reason it should work for you.

03 Plan

Pick one. It finds the slot.

The week fills itself around the rhythm you chose. Done posts turn green, planned ones wait in grey, and you can drag anything around.

04 Script

A script in your voice.

Hook, body, call to action. Change a line by typing or by talking, and it rewrites around you without losing the point.

05 Make

One script, four shapes.

The same script becomes a post, a carousel, a reel with word-by-word captions, and a voiceover. You watch Diw work through it, one honest line at a time.

PostCarouselReelVoiceover
06 Publish

Shaped for each channel, sent on time.

Square for the feed, vertical for TikTok and Shorts, a voice note and a caption for Telegram, an article for your blog, a thread for X. Each goes out at its own best hour.

07 See how it did

Then it tells you what worked.

Results come back per channel, in plain words, and the next morning's ideas lean toward what your people actually watched.
